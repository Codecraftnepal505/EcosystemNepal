About Us

EcoSmart Nepal is an environmental awareness project that encourages people to protect nature and keep Nepal clean and green.

Environmental Problems

🌫️ Air Pollution

Smoke from vehicles and factories makes the air unhealthy.

🗑️ Plastic Pollution

Plastic waste harms animals, rivers and the environment.

🌳 Deforestation

Cutting down trees destroys habitats and affects nature.

💧 Water Pollution

Waste and chemicals can make rivers and lakes unsafe.

Our Solutions

🌱 Plant Trees

Plant more trees and protect forests.

💧 Save Water

Use water carefully and avoid wasting it.

♻️ Reduce Plastic
