About EcoSystem Nepal

Understanding nature is the first step towards protecting it.

🌳🏔️🌿

Nature is our greatest treasure.

An ecosystem is a community of living organisms interacting with one another and with their non-living surroundings.

Nepal is rich in natural resources, forests, rivers, mountains and wildlife. These ecosystems support life and help maintain environmental balance.

EcoSystem Nepal aims to spread environmental awareness and encourage everyone to take responsibility for nature.

Discover Nature

Nepal's Natural Ecosystems

Explore some of the important parts of Nepal's environment.

🌲

Forests

Forests provide habitats for wildlife, absorb carbon dioxide, protect soil and support biodiversity.

🏔️

Mountains

Nepal's mountains are important natural landscapes and sources of freshwater for many communities.

🌊

Rivers and Lakes

Water ecosystems support aquatic organisms and provide water for people, farming and other activities.

🐅

Wildlife

Nepal is home to diverse wildlife, including Bengal tigers, one-horned rhinoceroses and red pandas.

🌾

Grasslands

Grasslands provide food and shelter for many animals and are important parts of natural ecosystems.

🦋

Biodiversity

Biodiversity means the variety of living organisms, including plants, animals and microorganisms.

Take Action

How Can We Protect Nature?

Simple habits can help make our environment cleaner and healthier.

🌳

Plant and Protect Trees

Plant trees and protect existing forests and green spaces.

♻️

Reduce, Reuse and Recycle

Use fewer disposable products and separate recyclable waste.

🚰

Save Water

Avoid wasting clean water and keep water sources free from pollution.

🐾

Protect Wildlife

Respect wild animals and help protect their natural habitats.

🧹

Keep Surroundings Clean

Use proper waste bins and take part in community clean-up activities.
